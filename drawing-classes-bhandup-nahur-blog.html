Government Drawing Exam Coaching

One of the most valuable offerings at Fitbeat is Govt Drawing Exam coaching. These exams are conducted by the Maharashtra State Board and are recognized for school certificates and college admissions into design and art courses.

Exam Level Who It's For
Elementary Drawing Exam Foundation Students from Class 5 onwards. Covers free-hand, memory & decorative art.
Intermediate Drawing Exam Advanced Students who have cleared Elementary. Still life, nature & geometric design.
Draw.C.E.T Exam Competitive Students applying to art & design colleges. Portfolio preparation included.

💡 Pro tip: The Elementary Drawing certificate is recognized across Maharashtra schools and adds significant value to a student's academic profile. Many parents enrol their children as early as Class 4–5 to get a head start.
