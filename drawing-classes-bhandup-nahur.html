What We Teach

Art Forms & Courses

From traditional Indian folk art to modern techniques — learn the art form that inspires you most.

Warli Painting

Ancient Maharashtra tribal art — geometric village life scenes using simple white figures on earthy backgrounds.

Calligraphy

English, Devnagari & decorative beautiful script styles — the art of beautiful, expressive writing.

Fabric Painting

Vibrant floral & abstract designs on fabric — wearable art that brings colour to your wardrobe.

Madhubani Art

Intricate Bihar folk art — mythology, nature & bold patterns in rich vibrant colours.

Hobby Drawing

Sketching, shading, pencil drawing and colouring for all ages. A fun creative outlet for children and adults.

Poster & Color Art

Poster colours, acrylic painting, pastel techniques — vibrant artwork that develops fine motor and creative skills.

Portrait (5-Month Course)

Master expressive portrait & character drawing — pencil shading, proportions and refined detailing over a structured 5-month program.

Govt Drawing Exams

Exam Coaching & Preparation

We provide focused coaching for Maharashtra Government Drawing Examinations — from beginners to advanced levels.

Elementary Drawing Exam

Foundation government drawing certificate. Ideal for school students in Bhandup, Nahur & Kanjurmarg. Full syllabus coverage & practice sets with mock tests.

Intermediate Drawing Exam

Advanced state-level certificate. Our students from Mulund, Nahur & Bhandup regularly qualify. Advanced techniques & composition/shading training.

Draw.C.E.T Exam

Drawing Common Entrance Test for art college admissions. Full CET pattern coaching — portfolio building, speed drawing & exam-oriented techniques.
